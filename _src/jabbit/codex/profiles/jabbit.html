-- Description --

A free spirited oddball with a lively personality who showed up out of nowhere.

Jabbit possesses a unique ability to freely morph his ears, almost as if they were another set of appendages. He can make the white tips loosely behave like hands, can stretch the entire ear to a limited degree as needed, and can compress them to then release it like a spring.

Furthermore, his ears are extra sensitive, enhancing his hearing and ability to feel movements around him, granting him greater awareness. Coupled with his quick reaction speeds, it essentially gives him a fast acting danger sense ability. Unfortunately, this sensitivity also tends to be rather overstimulating, especially in environments with lots of speech or obnoxious noise. He'll act tough in such situations, but it's quite clear that he's struggling.

Jabbit is surprisingly sturdy despite his, seemingly, flimsy build and with speed to boot, though he is far from the fastest around. He can hit like a truck with enough of a windup, although he tends to prefer a "death by a thousand cuts" strategy, either because he's confident in his stamina, or because he's messing with his opponent for fun. He often treats violent encounters like a game or a sparring match, even when he really shouldn't. Especially when he really shouldn't.

He's surprisingly observant despite how aloof or oblivious he can act sometimes, and the attention he gives to other's behaviour allows him to not only make very well informed decisions or extremely accurate predictions, but also enables him to mimic the behaviours and skills of others he observes fairly well. This hyper-awareness of his stems from both genuine admiration for others, as well as paranoia or general lack of faith.

Despite being mute, Jabbit's presence can be incredibly loud. Working alongside him feels more like having a sentient force of nature on your side, in terms of social dynamics anyway. He'll often take others around him into account and will usually listen to suggestions or commands, but he will never be bound to them. He makes this abundantly obvious with his stubborn actions and can be rather difficult to rein in when he's deadset on what he wants to do.

Jabbit can be super kind and friendly most of the time, yet he lacks capacity to trust even those closest to him unless they have jumped through a great number of hoops to partially earn it in his mind. He places little value in any words or promises made that lack the backing of genuine action. He very clearly means well and often wants to do good, despite his emotional distance and paranoia.

Sometimes when he's completely out of mental energy, he loses his usual green glow in his eyes as they become completely colourless and his attitude changes drastically. He becomes extremely uncaring and selfish, and often self destructive if pushed even further while in this state. However, he is very aware of his own limits and plans ahead, the result being that most who know him never even get to see this state in the first place and aren't even aware of his limits at all, leading to unrealistic expectations.

-- Traits --

  • Mute. Does not verbally communicate whatsoever.
  • Tends to act aloof, despite being somewhat kind and caring.
  • Somewhat of a clown.
  • Surprisingly high adaptability and endurance.
  • Very fast reaction speeds. He is very reaction based in general.
  • Hyper aware of other's behaviour/emotions. Sometimes chooses to ignore them anyway.
  • Action oriented. Doesn't place much value in words.
  • Can replicate skills of others rather quickly simply through observation.
  • Greatly enjoys being physically challenged. To a fault.
  • Would rather be shot with a gun than stabbed with a knife.

-- Design Notes --

General

  • Cartoony/slightly exaggerated style. His physique can be squashed and stretched to a limited degree and are not rigid.
  • Somewhat inspired by Sonic's character designs but not quite as "Mickey Mouse" like regarding proportions and the style of limbs.
  • He has soft and fluffy fur.
  • While he is canonically male, his appearance is a little more on the androgynous side.

Ears

  • He can morph his ears and shift their location on his head somewhat. They are not locked in place.
  • When in a "neutral" position when drawn, his ears tend to behave similarly to Mickey Mouse's ears, or Goku's hair, where they are often drawn in a sort of "billboard" fashion where they will remain in a set pose and face the "camera" even when the head is rotated away.
  • Can use his ears like another set of limbs if he wants.
  • Can slightly adjust the white tips of the ears to be more "hand-like" but it's a far cry from being like actual hands.
  • His ears do not have an opening/pinna normally, but since he can morph them, drawing his ears to be more accurate to real rabbit ears is still considered valid.
  • If he is drawn with an opening/pinna then it absolutely must not have visible flesh coloured parts. The insides must match the same black/white scheme as usual.
  • His ability to use his ears like limbs is inspired by Buneary from Pokemon, and a concept design for Tangle from the Sonic IDW series (Tangle was originally a hare in that concept with ears that could morph into very strong and large "hands". The concept design mentioned was made by Tyson Hesse.)

Paws/Hands/Feet

  • Jabbit completely lacks paw pads of any kind. It's all fur.
  • Jabbit completely lacks any sort of claws or nails.

Face

  • Eyes are normally green and produce a very dim amount of light which is only noticeable in darkness.
  • Depending on emotional state, his eyes can lose their colour and the irises will become black or dim based on severity.
  • Based on context, or vibes I guess, his eyes can be drawn without the sclera, as if he doesn't even have eyeballs and the irises are just superimposed onto the black fur where the sockets would have been. Cartoon logic.
  • When closing the eyes, I strongly suggest using white for the lines to contrast with the black fur.
  • I don't normally draw him with typical rabbit teeth but it would still be valid to do so.

Body

  • The portion of chest covered by the white fur has extra thickness to it and tufts outwards more than the rest.

Tail

  • His large tail is super cushiony! Perhaps even bouncy.

-- Trivia --

As well as being the titular character for the game, Jabbit also serves as my mascot character. I tend to use him a lot as avatars online, although I don't really consider him to be a sona.

---

Most pixel art of Jabbit was created using Aseprite, and his colour pallette is taken from this colour pallette from LOSPEC. As is the case with all sprites in the game.

---

The names "Jabbit" and "Jack" were both given nicknames by others he's well aquatinted with. He's never objected to these and has never even attempted to give his actual name. He probably prefers them over whatever his real name is.

---

Many years ago (aprox. 2022, maybe?), Jabbit started as a concept for a mascot character to represent me online. They weren't called Jabbit initially, and they weren't even a furry character. They were an androgynous human character who wore a rabbit themed hoodie, with ears similar to Jabbit's on the hood. The whole "Jack" naming used later actually started out as this concept character used various wires hidden in their clothing to hack into things, or just for general electrical shenanigans. After I decided that I wanted to make a platformer, I went more in the direction of an anthropomorphic rabbit character, being inspired by some fan characters I saw on Twitter that gave new life to that one rabbit concept design for Sonic The Hedgehog. (I believe the fan OC based on it was called Rush? It's been a while.)

---

The design you see for Jabbit now came from a very rushed mockup of an in-game sprite concept I whipped up in a matter of minutes, right before Art Fight 2025. A friend really wanted me to get involved but I had no finished characters! So I just doodled some sprites, sat on a few for a while, and eventually the one that would be used as the base for the canon design grew on me, so I just went with it.

---

Allegedly, Jabbit has been seen wearing a maid outfit... I don't have evidence of this claim but it's what I've heard from some. Slay?
