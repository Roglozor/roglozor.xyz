Projects

-- Jabbit --

Jabbit is my first ever video game, developed using Godot Engine. It's a simple 2D action platformer, focused mainly on multiplayer PVP for now, as well as supporting custom addons like character skins and stages. It's still in development privately, but I'm planning to reveal it this year once I get my house in order IRL.

The titular character also happens to be my mascot OC, however he is not a sona in any form. I have some mid pixel art of him in my Gallery created by myself, as well as on Toyhouse.
