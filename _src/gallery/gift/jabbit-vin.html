7th Jul 2025

Jabbit, drawn in an Art Fight attack from my friend, Vin. This is the first ever gift art of Jabbit, or any of my "modern" characters that I've actually decided to make public. Funnily enough, this image helped me figure out how I really wanted Jabbit to look later on, mainly the increased fluffiness versus the original, flat and bland ref sheet this drawing was based on. I adore this piece and I'm still impressed with the fact that anyone could actually interpret my original ref sheet at all.
