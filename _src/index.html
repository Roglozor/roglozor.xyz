About Me

I'm a British (derogatory) hobbyist of creating various digital media, primarily a game developer. Apparently I'm an INFP-T, I'm currently in my 20s, and I'm autistic. Normally I don't like bringing attention to my condition, but it's a very large part of who I am and effects my life and work greatly, so please understand this.

Here's a few bits about my workflow:

  • My favourite programing language is C and I like making my own engines.
  • I also have experience with Godot Engine and many prototypes were made with it initially.

Jabbit

My first video game! A singleplayer focused action platformer, staring my mascot OC of the same name. Explore the depths of Imaginaria, a mysterious but whimsical plane granting physical form to thought, emotion, and soul. As Jabbit, dodge, parry, and bounce from certain projectiles whilst you dash through stages and arenas, exploiting weakened foes and harnessing their power as your own.

What started as a far simpler PVP game inspired by the Mario Vs Luigi mode in NSMB has evolved into a much larger project with a single player campaign, taking much more inspiration from a bunch of my most favourite games ever. Despite this new focus, Jabbit will still focus plenty of attention to the multiplayer PVP experience I originally imagined, as well as built in support for custom character skins, stages, and eventually an official modding API using Lua.

This game is still being worked on, so I don't have much to show yet!
