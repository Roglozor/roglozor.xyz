-- Description --

Jabbit is completely mute and prefers to let his actions do the talking. Despite acting aloof, he seems to be rather kind and considerate and has a deep emotional attachment to his companions, but still prefers to do things his way.

The long, ear-like appendages on his head are incredibly sensitive to vibrations and can be somewhat morphed into various shapes and sizes, most often being used as a stronger pair of hands.

The large and rather fluffy tail seems to be connected to another space entirely and can store a rather comical mass of objects. Only items that can fit through its diameter can enter or exit this space.

Recently, he has been observed to have a similar ability using the white sections of his ears, specifically using them to temporarily "absorb" the abilities of weakened foes. It is thought that he may be able to channel various taken powers into other such coloured sections of his body.

Despite appearances and his given nickname, Jabbit's genetic makeup is a total anomaly and he has no known relatives.

-- Trivia --

As well as being the titular character for the game, Jabbit also serves as my mascot character. I tend to use him a lot as avatars online, although I don't really consider him to be a sona.

---

Most pixel art of Jabbit was created using Aseprite, and his colour pallette is taken from this colour pallette from LOSPEC. As is the case with all sprites in the game.

---

The names "Jabbit" and "Jack" were both given nicknames by others he's well acquainted with. He's never objected to these and has never even attempted to give his actual name. He probably prefers them over whatever his real name is.

---

Many years ago (aprox. 2022, maybe?), Jabbit started as a concept for a mascot character to represent me online. They weren't called Jabbit initially, and they weren't even a furry character. They were an androgynous human character who wore a rabbit themed hoodie, with ears similar to Jabbit's on the hood. The whole "Jack" naming used later actually started out as this concept character used various wires hidden in their clothing to hack into things, or just for general electrical shenanigans. After I decided that I wanted to make a platformer, I went more in the direction of an anthropomorphic rabbit character, being inspired by some fan characters I saw on Twitter that gave new life to that one rabbit concept design for Sonic The Hedgehog. (I believe the fan OC based on it was called Rush? It's been a while.)

---

The design you see for Jabbit now came from a very rushed mockup of an in-game sprite concept I whipped up in a matter of minutes, right before Art Fight 2025. A friend really wanted me to get involved but I had no finished characters! So I just doodled some sprites, sat on a few for a while, and eventually the one that would be used as the base for the canon design grew on me, so I just went with it.

---

Allegedly, Jabbit has been seen wearing a maid outfit... I don't have evidence of this claim but it's what I've heard from some. Slay?
